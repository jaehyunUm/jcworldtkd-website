Why I Created Martial Arts Studio

In many martial arts schools, testing is often based on subjective scores given by masters. Unfortunately, these scores don’t always reflect a student’s true growth. Students may pass or fail, but they are often left wondering: “Am I really improving?” I wanted to change that. Growth has always been the core value I emphasize in my teaching, so I created Martial Arts Studio to provide students with objective data that makes their progress visible and meaningful. With this system, every test is more than just a grade — it becomes a measurement of growth. Students can see how their speed, strength, endurance, and skills improve over time. Instead of relying on paper records or vague feedback, they now have clear evidence of their development. This approach not only motivates students to train harder but also builds their confidence and self-esteem. By seeing their own progress, students begin to understand that perseverance leads to results, and results lead to growth. My goal with Martial Arts Studio is simple: to create a testing system that is fair, motivating, and focused on true growth — so that every student can feel proud of how far they’ve come and excited about how far they can go.

Testing System

In our testing system, Taekwondo techniques are evaluated based on the five key components of physical fitness.
This approach ensures that students develop balanced skills in both technique and physical conditioning.

Badge / Ranking System

With every improvement, students earn badges for specific skill areas — building motivation and confidence.

Our ranking system also encourages healthy competition, helping students set goals and push themselves further.
